Verbatim exports of the owner's spreadsheet ledger (Google Sheets "Cigars",
Purchases tab — the authoritative tab; others are out of sync). Seed data
for reconciling `purchases` beyond the mkdocs table and, later, the R13
inventory.

- `purchases-2026-08-27.csv` — 103 rows, 2025-08-06 → 2025-11-10. Supersets
  the imported purchase-history.md (92 rows); the delta is largely the
  late-2025 Cuban boxes. Known quirks: "Rockey Patel" typo, one `???` brand,
  Vitola/Size blank + PPS filled on the Cuban half, Humidor Data sometimes
  holds status words (Backordered/Stuck), Aging is free text.
